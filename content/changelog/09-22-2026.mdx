- **Testimonials Section v7.** A title and short description sit on the left, and three quote cards stack on the right with small alternating tilts. Hovering a card straightens it and lifts it to the front. Each card ends with an avatar, a role, and a company wordmark that swaps for light and dark mode. Below `lg` the cards fall back to a plain vertical list.
- **Pricing Section v5.** Hobby, Pro, and Enterprise share one bordered grid, and CSS subgrid keeps prices, descriptions, and feature lists level across all three columns. Pro is tinted and tagged Popular. Every feature has its own Tabler icon, and each plan ends with a pill button.

Previews on block and template pages now have a drag handle on the right edge. Drag it to any width between full size and mobile, which is the minimum. The Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile buttons highlight when the width matches a preset.
