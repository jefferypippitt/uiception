Templates sit next to blocks as a new install path: a complete Next.js app rather than a single section, installed with `shadcn init --template`.

- **Portfolio v1.** A science themed starter for writers, set in Geist Mono for a typewriter feel, with MDX powered writing and book pages, a Saturn decoration, and a Basin contact form.
