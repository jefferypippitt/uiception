- **Stats Section v5.** Bars turn green, amber, or red by speed, and dashed lines mark the typical and 99% times. Hovering a bar shows the share of requests finished by then.
- **Stats Section v6.** Each tile's 10 by 10 grid lights only its channel's squares, in the spots they hold in the full 100. The lit squares fade on a 3.6 second loop that sweeps diagonally.
- **Contact Section v1.** A large "Let's collaborate" title and YouTube, LinkedIn, and X links are on the left, with a name, email, company, and message form on the right.
- **Partners Section v1.** A centered title is above a three column grid of AI company wordmarks, and each wordmark has a separate dark mode version.
