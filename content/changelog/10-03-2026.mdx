<New>

- **Partners Section v2.** The title is on the left and a framed three by two grid is on the right. Every 1.6 seconds one cell blurs its wordmark out and the next of twelve rises in.
- **Partners Section v3.** Eight social networks are listed by icon and name in a two column grid at up to 60px. The heading words brighten one by one as the section scrolls in.
- **Partners Section v4.** Eight infrastructure logos are in one static row with no cards or animation. Odd logos move up and even logos move down, so the row zigzags.
- **CTA Section v7.** A Windows Terminal window has a copy button beside each command. The PowerShell command quotes the registry name, because PowerShell reads a leading @ as splatting.

</New>

<Improved>

- **CTA Section v7.** The arrow keys switch between the PowerShell and WSL tabs.

</Improved>

<Fixed>

- **CTA Section v7.** Screen readers announce when the command is copied.
- **Partners Section v2.** The grid no longer flashes on load, and it now draws in after the title and description.

</Fixed>
